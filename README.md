One text message (106 characters) from the writer's future self, written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e) "Texts From Your Future Self".

## The entry

> Don't reply to this. I know you want to. I did. It created a paradox and now I owe taxes in two timelines.

Ten candidates were drafted and compared against the judge's five criteria (a real future-self text first, then a joke that lands on first read, then a specific voice, then every character earning its place, then a fresh angle — not broke, not tired): time-travel Wi-Fi, the dog Biscuit, tattoo/haircut/Dave, knees filing a complaint, learning to cook, the no-reply paradox, the wedding and the in-laws, the meeting that could have been an email, the toaster hack, living to 87 looking for glasses. The no-reply paradox won: it is the only candidate that uses the medium itself (a text about replying to a text), its twist lands twice (a paradox, then taxes in two timelines), only a future self could warn you not to reply, and at 106 characters no word is wasted. Runner-up was the dog Biscuit — warm and specific, but its punchline is gentler.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (absolute path, as the competition requires) after checking the gate inside the program: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, a single line, not only whitespace, and byte length equal to character length (pure ASCII, which also helps the byte tie-break).
